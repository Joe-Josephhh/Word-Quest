GAME GUIDELINES

How to Play

1

Answer the Question

Read the Independence Day question carefully and identify the answer.

2

Find the Hidden Word

Find the answer hidden in the 15 × 15 letter grid.

3

Select the Answer

Click and drag from the first letter to the last letter.

4

One 40-Minute Timer

You get 40 minutes for all 30 questions. The timer continues when you move between questions.

5

Correct Answer: +5

Find the correct answer without using the hint and receive 5 points.

6

One Hint Only

You have only one hint during the entire game. It turns one letter of the correct answer green.

7

Correct With Hint: +1

If you use the hint and then correctly select the answer, you receive 1 point.

8

Wrong Answer: −1

An incorrect selection deducts 1 point from your score.

9

Skip: −1

You may skip a difficult question, but skipping deducts 1 point.

10

Complete the Quest

Try to solve all 30 questions before the 40-minute timer expires.
